# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who runs the platform. Only an Admin decides Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: the team, staff (for Admins specifically)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once. Once an Admin decides it, the decision is final, and an Admin never decides their own Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, "the team" (for whoever decides — a Team is a customer's group of seats)

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and the learner loses access to the course at that moment (their progress is kept in case they buy again). A Refund Request on a team licence can't be approved once any of its seats has been claimed, and approving it makes the unclaimed seats unusable.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a reason the learner is told. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund on an approved Refund Request where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
