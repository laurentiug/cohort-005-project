# Cadence

An online course platform where Learners buy courses, work through their lessons, and can ask for their money back.

## Language

### People and buying

**Learner**:
A person who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Student, buyer, customer ("user" only for the account itself)

**Purchase**:
A Learner's payment for one course. A Purchase whose Refund Request was Approved is **Refunded**, and the Learner's Enrollment in that course ends.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Enrollment**:
A Learner's access to a course. It usually comes from a Purchase but can exist without one, for example on a free course or a Team Seat.
_Avoid_: Access, subscription

**Team Seat**:
A place on a team's licence that a Learner claims. It gives them an Enrollment without a Purchase of their own.
_Avoid_: Coupon, licence seat

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Staff, support, "us"

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's ask, with a written reason, to get their money back for a Purchase. It is Requested until an Admin Approves or Declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback, refund (when meaning the request)

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase may have a Refund Request raised for it right now.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, can cancel

**Refund Window**:
The period after a Purchase during which a Refund Request may be raised, counted in whole calendar days.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Amount**:
How much a Refund returns, which is always what the Learner actually paid for the Purchase.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price

**Refund**:
Money returned to a Learner for a Purchase, which only happens when a Refund Request is Approved. A Declined Refund Request produces no Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute the Learner raises through their bank or card issuer, outside Cadence's own process. It is not a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Using it for a Refund Request or a Refund
